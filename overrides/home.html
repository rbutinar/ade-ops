Operate your analytics platform — people and AI agents, in control.

ade-ops is an open-source framework for analytics teams working across Databricks, Microsoft Fabric, and Power BI. Author once, deploy everywhere — and every change to a live environment is shown as a diff and confirmed before it's written. Start on a single project; scale to how your whole team operates.

What you get

Author once, deploy everywhere

A single source of truth in src/, adapted per environment with overlays. The same engine treats Databricks, Fabric and Power BI the same way.

Diff before every push

Remote environments are authoritative. You see exactly what changes before it's written — no silent overrides.

People and AI agents, together

Usable from a plain CLI, and built for AI coding assistants to drive the work — with a human confirmation on anything that touches a live environment.

AI-agnostic — no lock-in

Works with Claude Code, GitHub Copilot and Codex. The same open framework, whichever assistant your team runs.

Part of the ADE family

Independent, composable tools for analytics data engineering — use either on its own or together. ade-catalog maps what exists and how it's connected; ade-ops operates what you run and where you deploy it. Map the territory, then operate it.
